FAQ

How do I build?

During the day, tap an empty plot and choose a building: Archer Tower, Brazier, Barracks or Farmstead. Plots on the enemy path only take Palisades. Tap an existing building to upgrade it (three tiers) or demolish it for a 50% refund.

How do I control the Warden?

At night, tap or drag anywhere on the map to move your Warden. He fights foes near him and slowly heals when standing by the hearth. If he falls, he returns after a short while.

What does warmth do?

Frost Shamans can freeze your buildings, but anything inside the warmth of the hearth or a brazier cannot be frozen. You can see the snow melt inside the warm areas.

How do I earn gold?

You earn gold at every dawn you survive. Farmsteads add extra gold each dawn. Demolishing a building refunds half its cost.

Which enemies will I face?

Frostlings, fast Ice Wolves, Ice Trolls that smash buildings, Frost Shamans that freeze them, and Rime Bats that fly over walls (only Archer Towers can hit flyers). Night 10 brings the Ice Colossus.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your game is saved automatically at every dawn, so you can close the app and choose Continue later. Saves stay on your device. The game has no accounts or cloud storage, so deleting the app also deletes its progress.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open Options from the main menu (or during a game) and switch Sound Effects, Music or Haptics on or off. Your choices are saved on your device.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Hearthhold works fully offline. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

The game feels slow. What can I try?

Close other apps and restart the game, and check that your device isn't in a battery-saving mode. Updating your device's OS can also help. If it keeps happening, email us.
